Roadster

It drives up a wall. Then it lets go.

Watch the run See it on tesla.com
Scroll

Straight Up

Fans under the floor pull the car into the concrete, so grip stops depending on gravity. It drives up the wall and keeps going past vertical.

Let Go

At the top it leaves the lip upside down. Cold-gas thrusters along the body roll it upright on the way down.

Cold Gas

Four jets under the floor catch the fall and set the car down on its wheels.

<1.0s 0–60 mph with rear thrusters

Hidden Aero

No visible headlights and no wings bolted on. Everything that moves air sits flush with the body until it is needed.

Under the Skin

Lift off the canopy and the shell. What is left is a battery, three motors, two air tanks and a ring of nozzles.

Light Wheels

Less mass to spin up means the car answers sooner. Behind each wheel: brake, upright, wishbones and spring.

Pilot Only

A yoke, a fixed seat and only the information the driver needs.

Performance
