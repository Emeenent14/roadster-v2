TAKE IT APART.

Canopy off. Shell off. Underneath: a battery, two air tanks, three motors and a ring of nozzles.

ACT 05 — DETAILS

LIGHT WHEELS.

Thin spokes, very little mass. Less weight to spin up means the car answers faster.
